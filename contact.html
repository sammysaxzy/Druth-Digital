Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does installation take?

Standard residential installation typically takes 2-4 hours. Business installations may take longer depending on complexity. We'll schedule a convenient appointment time for you.

Do you offer contracts?

We offer both contract and month-to-month options. Contract plans often come with better pricing or free installation. Contact our sales team to learn about current promotions.

What equipment is included?

All plans include a modem/router. Higher-tier plans include premium equipment. You can also use your own compatible equipment if preferred.

How can I check availability?

You can check availability by entering your address on our website, calling our sales team, or using our coverage map. We'll let you know which services are available at your location.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee for new customers. If you're not satisfied with our service within the first 30 days, we'll provide a full refund.

Do you support small businesses?

Yes! We offer specialized business solutions for companies of all sizes, from small home offices to large enterprises. Contact our business team for customized solutions.
